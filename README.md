An original open-world crime game built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty.
Welcome to Porto Serena, a golden-hour coastal city of neon avenues, palms, a
beach and a marina — every name, character, story and asset invented for this
game. Explore on foot or steal any car and drive; traffic flows, pedestrians
stroll, flee and fight back, and the police answer a 0–5-star wanted level with
pursuit, ramming and a bust meter. Work through Rosa's four-mission story chain
and repeatable errands, fight with fists or a pistol, and keep an eye on the
live minimap. Title and pause screens, a full HUD, synthesized engine, siren
and music audio, and a localStorage save round it out. Runs offline in desktop
Chrome with keyboard and mouse; three.js is vendored, nothing is fetched.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

then open the printed local address — or open `game/index.html` directly.

Controls: WASD move / drive · E enter or steal a car · Shift sprint · Space
handbrake · mouse aim, click to punch or shoot · Esc pause.
